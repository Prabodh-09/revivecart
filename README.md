#ReviveCart
ReviveCart is a web-based platform built to reduce food and pharmaceutical waste by reselling products nearing their expiry date at discounted prices. The platform connects sellers with buyers who are looking for affordable options, ensuring that usable products don’t go to waste.

#Tech Stack:
->Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript.
->Backend: Node.js, Express.js.
->Database: MongoDB.
->Deployment: Hostinger VPS (in future).
